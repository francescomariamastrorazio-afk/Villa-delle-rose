The Estate & Vulture Regional Park

A serene natural sanctuary framed by the volcanic lakes of Monticchio and ancient chestnut woods.

Panoramic Outdoor Grounds • Vulture Regional Park

Set in the heart of Monticchio, inside the protected Vulture Regional Park, Villa delle Rose blends the unspoiled beauty of an ancient volcanic landscape with the refined warmth of a private estate designed for destination weddings.

Crafted for multi-day celebrations, intimate hospitality, and authentic Southern Italian charm, the estate allows couples, families, and international guests to unwind across sunlit terraces, shaded gardens, and scenic al fresco gathering spaces.

Landscape Heritage & Hospitality

  • Volcanic Nature & Lakes: Moments away from the twin Lakes of Monticchio, immersed in tranquil woodland air.
  • Multi-Day Destination Weddings: Bespoke weekend itineraries spanning welcome dinners, wedding rituals, and farewell brunches.
  • Private Panoramic Terraces: Versatile outdoor settings bathed in natural light from morning to golden hour.

The Courtyard: Civil Vows & Indian Mandap Ceremonies

Mandap & Floral Styling

Romantic Civil & Symbolic Vows

The sweeping open-air courtyard welcomes civil and symbolic ceremonies with effortless grace, offering a dramatic floral aisle, tailored guest seating, and the picturesque facade and woodlands as a natural backdrop.

Grand Indian Religious Rituals

Generous, airy proportions allow for authentic Indian wedding ceremonies featuring a custom Mandap, ceremonial thrones, sacred fire arrangements, and lush floral garlands in a warm, welcoming setting.
